Hover any item and see how many each of your characters has, and where. Alt Inventory adds a few lines to the item tooltip with a count per character across Bags, Character Bank, Mail and Warband Bank. No more logging in to three alts to find your herbs. It also keeps every character's gold, the currencies you can transfer between them, and how long you've played each one.

New: Every Version of WoW
One download for Retail, WoW Forever, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Burning Crusade Anniversary and Classic Era (Hardcore and Season of Discovery too). On Classic it counts your keyring and every currency per character, and the Warband Bank shows wherever the game has one.

Light and focused: a minimal alternative to TradeSkillMaster's inventory tooltip, without its memory footprint. No setup needed.

New: Play Time
A Play time tab in the Alt Inventory window shows how long you've played, by character, class, role or faction, as bars with each one's share and the total. Each character's time is read when you log into it, without the usual /played lines in chat, and keeps counting while you play.

Gold and Currencies
Hover the minimap button for every character's gold, the Warband Bank's and the total. Hover a currency that can be moved between characters to see how much each one has, or search for it by name, before you use the game's Transfer.

Search All Your Characters
Where did you leave those potions? Click the magnifier in your bags' search box or the Alt Inventory button on the minimap (or type /altinv search) and type part of an item's name. Every match across all your characters, their banks and mail, and the Warband Bank is listed with a count per character. Filter by character or by place, sort by name or by total, and shift-click an item to link it in chat. Whatever you'd typed in the bag search comes along with you.

What It Tracks

  • Bags on every character.
  • Character Bank on every character.
  • Mail: items still attached to letters in each character's mailbox.
  • Warband Bank, shown once as its own line since it's shared by your whole account.
  • Gold on every character and in the Warband Bank.
  • Transferable currencies on every character.
  • Time played on every character, with its class, role and faction.

Nothing else on purpose: no auction house or vendor prices, so it stays small and fast.

Know When a Count Is Old
Bank, Mail and Warband Bank counts more than a day old show their age, for example Bank: 20 (12d ago), so you know when it's time to visit that bank again.

Getting Started
The game only lets an addon see the character you're logged in on, so each character needs a quick visit:

  1. Log in on each character once with the addon installed. That records its bags, gold, currencies and time played.
  2. Open their bank (and the Warband Bank) and their mailbox once.

That's it. From then on every character sees everyone's items, and counts update whenever you play them. Altoholic and TSM work the same way, because it's how the game works.

Commands

  • /altinv: rescan now and list every tracked character with their gold and when their bank and mailbox were last opened.
  • /altinv search: open the window (Search and Play time). /altinv find potion opens it searching for "potion".
  • /altinv remove Name (or Name-Realm): forget a deleted or transferred character.
  • /altinv minimap: show or hide the minimap button.
