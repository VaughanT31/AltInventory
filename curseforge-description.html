Hover any item and see how many each of your characters has, and where. Alt Inventory adds a few lines to the item tooltip with a count per character across Bags, Character Bank and Warband Bank. No more logging in to three alts to find your herbs.

Alt Inventory              Total: 47
Fatheed                    Bags: 12, Bank: 20
Bankalt                    Bank: 10 (12d ago)
Warband Bank               5

Light and focused: a minimal alternative to TradeSkillMaster's inventory tooltip, without its memory footprint. No setup, no window to open.

What It Tracks

  • Bags on every character.
  • Character Bank on every character.
  • Warband Bank, shown once as its own line since it's shared by your whole account.

Nothing else on purpose: no auction house, mail or vendor prices, so it stays small and fast.

Know When a Count Is Old
Bank and Warband Bank counts more than a day old show their age, for example Bank: 20 (12d ago), so you know when it's time to visit that bank again.

Getting Started
The game only lets an addon see the character you're logged in on, so each character needs a quick visit:

  1. Log in on each character once with the addon installed.
  2. Open their bank (and the Warband Bank) once.

That's it. From then on every character sees everyone's items, and counts update whenever you play them. Altoholic and TSM work the same way, because it's how the game works.

Commands

  • /altinv: rescan now and list every tracked character with when their bank was last opened.
  • /altinv remove Name (or Name-Realm): forget a deleted or transferred character.
