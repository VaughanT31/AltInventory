Hover any item and see how many each of your characters has, and where. Alt Inventory adds a few lines to the item tooltip with a count per character across Bags, Character Bank, Mail and Warband Bank. No more logging in to three alts to find your herbs. It also keeps every character's gold, the currencies you can transfer between them, and how long you've played each one.

New in 2.0: A Whole New Look
The Alt Inventory window has been redesigned from the ground up: wider, darker and easier to read, the same on every version of the game. Your characters sit down the left with their class colour and item level, search results are cards that show who holds most of an item, and there's a tab for your characters, your play time and your watch lists.

New: See Any Character's Bags
Click a character in the window to see their bags, bank, mail and the Warband Bank as icon grids, without logging in on them. Hover an item for its tooltip, and shift-click to link it.

Every Version of WoW
One download for Retail, WoW Forever, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Burning Crusade Anniversary and Classic Era (Hardcore and Season of Discovery too). On WoW Forever your characters are kept apart by their full name, so two with the same first name never get mixed up. On Classic it counts your keyring and every currency per character, and the Warband Bank shows wherever the game has one.

Light and focused: a minimal alternative to TradeSkillMaster's inventory tooltip, without its memory footprint. No setup needed.

New: Weekly
Every character's week on one page: the Great Vault's raid, dungeon and world rows, the Mythic+ keys done this week and raid lockouts with bosses down and when they reset. See at a glance which alt still needs a key for the vault, and which ones have rewards waiting after the reset. Raid lockouts show on WoW Forever and Classic too. Type /altinv weekly.

New: Gold Over Time
A chart of your gold over 30 days, 90 days or a year, for all your gold, the Warband Bank or any one character, with each character's change underneath. Type /altinv gold.

Watch Lists
Keep an eye on the items you care about across every character. Consumables and Trade goods are built in, and on Retail so is Lumber, with your Essence of Lumber on top and how much lumber it's worth at the neighborhood lumberjack. Make your own lists too: click New list and drop items on it. Type /altinv watch to open them.

Characters
A Characters tab shows every character at a glance: your total gold, highest item level and cooldowns ready on top, then a table you can sort by item level, gold, bag space, reagent bag or last played. Hover a character for concentration (worked out for the time they've been away), Moxie, profession cooldowns and this patch's currencies, and click one to see their bags.

Reagent Finder
Beside the profession window, a small panel shows which of your other characters and your Warband Bank hold the materials for the recipe or crafting order on screen. A button beside the window's tabs turns it on and off, and it stacks with Patron Planner's and Auction Coach's panels instead of covering them. Shift-drag a panel by its title to move the whole column, and Shift-right-click it to put it back; the spot is remembered. Retail and WoW Forever.

Play Time
A Play time tab in the Alt Inventory window shows how long you've played, by character, class, role or faction, as bars with each one's share and the total. Each character's time is read when you log into it, without the usual /played lines in chat, and keeps counting while you play.

Gold and Currencies
Hover the minimap button for every character's gold, the Warband Bank's and the total. Hover a currency that can be moved between characters to see how much each one has, or search for it by name, before you use the game's Transfer.

Search All Your Characters
Where did you leave those potions? Click the magnifier in your bags' search box or the Alt Inventory button on the minimap (or type /altinv search) and type part of an item's name. Every match across all your characters, their banks and mail, and the Warband Bank is shown as a card with the total and who holds it. Filter by place, sort by name or by most held, and shift-click an item to link it in chat. Whatever you'd typed in the bag search comes along with you.

What It Tracks

  • Bags on every character.
  • Character Bank on every character.
  • Mail: items still attached to letters in each character's mailbox.
  • Warband Bank, shown once as its own line since it's shared by your whole account.
  • Gold on every character and in the Warband Bank.
  • Transferable currencies on every character.
  • Time played on every character, with its class, role and faction.

No auction house or vendor prices on purpose, so it stays small and fast.

Know When a Count Is Old
Bank, Mail and Warband Bank counts more than a day old show their age, for example Bank: 20 (12d ago), so you know when it's time to visit that bank again.

Expiring Mail Warning
Mail with items or gold that expires within 3 days gets a warning when you log in, on any character, so nothing gets sent back or deleted. Tooltips show it too, for example Mail: 3 (expires in 1d 4h). /altinv mail lists it any time.

Getting Started
The game only lets an addon see the character you're logged in on, so each character needs a quick visit:

  1. Log in on each character once with the addon installed. That records its bags, gold, currencies and time played.
  2. Open their bank (and the Warband Bank) and their mailbox once.

That's it. From then on every character sees everyone's items, and counts update whenever you play them. Altoholic and TSM work the same way, because it's how the game works.

Commands

  • /altinv: rescan now and list every tracked character with their gold and when their bank and mailbox were last opened.
  • /altinv search: open the window (Search, Characters, Play time and Watch lists). /altinv find potion opens it searching for "potion".
  • /altinv characters: open the Characters tab.
  • /altinv watch: open your watch lists.
  • /altinv weekly: every character's vault, keys and raid lockouts this week.
  • /altinv gold: your gold over time.
  • /altinv lumber: open the Lumber watch list (Retail).
  • /altinv mail: list mail with items or gold expiring in the next 3 days. /altinv mailwarn turns the login warning off or on.
  • /altinv reagents: turn the reagent finder off or on.
  • /altinv remove Name (or Name-Realm): forget a deleted, renamed or transferred character.
  • /altinv minimap: show or hide the minimap button.
  • /altinv debug: if something isn't working, type this and paste what it prints into a comment.
