# Item tooltips

*What the Alt Inventory lines in an item's tooltip mean, and why some counts show their age.*

Hover any item and Alt Inventory adds its lines under the game's own text:

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Alt Inventory, Total: 47** | How many you have across every character and the Warband Bank |
| **Fatheed: Bags 12, Bank 20** | One line per character that holds it, and where |
| **Warband Bank: 5** | The Warband Bank is shared by all characters, so it has one line of its own |

The places are **Bags**, **Bank** (the character's own bank), **Mail** (items attached to letters waiting in the mailbox) and **Warband Bank**.

### Why a count shows "(12d ago)"

Bank, mail and Warband Bank counts only update when you open them. When a count is more than a day old, the tooltip shows its age, for example `Bank: 20 (12d ago)`. Open that bank or mailbox on that character and the count is fresh again.

### A character is missing

They haven't logged in with Alt Inventory installed yet, or don't hold the item. Log in on them once. If a character has been deleted or moved to another realm, remove it with `/altinv remove Name`.
