# Searching your characters

*Find any item across every character, bank and mailbox, and see any character's bags without logging in.*

### Open the window

- Click the **magnifier** in your bags' search box.
- Click the **Alt Inventory button** on the minimap (it's also in the minimap's addon list).
- Type `/altinv search`, or `/altinv find potion` to open it already searching for "potion".

The window opens on the **Search** tab. Drag it by the title bar to move it, and it stays where you put it.

### Your characters

Every character is listed down the left, grouped by realm (your current realm first), in their class colour with their item level. **All characters** at the top is the search across everyone.

### Search

Type part of an item's name. Each match is a card: the item in its quality colour, how many you have in total, a bar showing who holds most of it, and every character that holds it with where.

- **Filter by place:** All, Bags, Bank, Mail, Warband, or **Currencies** to search currencies instead of items.
- **Sort** by name or by most held.
- **Shift-click** a card to link the item in chat.
- **Type a number** to search by item ID.
- **Drop an item** on the search box to search for exactly that item.

The Warband filter only shows on game versions that have a Warband Bank. See [Game versions](Game-versions.md).

### See a character's bags

Click a character on the left to see what they hold, without logging in on them. The top shows their class, realm, item level, when they were last on and their gold. Pick **Bags**, **Bank**, **Mail** or **Warband bank** to see that place as a grid of item icons.

![One character's bags, with their bank, mail and the Warband Bank a click away](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ai-search.webp)
*One character's bags, with their bank, mail and the Warband Bank a click away*

- **Hover** an item for its tooltip, and **shift-click** to link it.
- The bottom right says when that place was last read. A bank or mailbox is read when you open it on that character.
- Click **All characters** to go back to the search.
