# Searching your characters

*Find any item across every character, bank and mailbox by typing part of its name.*

### Open the search window

- Click the **magnifier** in your bags' search box.
- Click the **Alt Inventory button** on the minimap (it's also in the minimap's addon list).
- Type `/altinv search`, or `/altinv find potion` to open it already searching for "potion".

### Search

Type part of an item's name. Every match across all your characters is listed, with a count per character and per place.

- **Filter by character** to see only what one character holds.
- **Filter by place:** Bags, Bank, Mail, Warband Bank, or **Currencies** to search currencies instead of items.
- **Sort** by name or by total.
- **Shift-click** a result to link it in chat.
- **Type a number** to search by item ID.
- **Drop an item** on the search box to search for exactly that item.

The Warband Bank filter only shows on game versions that have a Warband Bank. See [Game versions](Game-versions.md).
