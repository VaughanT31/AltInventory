# Watch lists

*Keep an eye on the items you care about across every character: consumables, trade goods, lumber and your own lists.*

Open the **Watch lists** tab in the Alt Inventory window, or type `/altinv watch`. Pick a list on the left, and each item on it shows as a card: how many you have in total, a bar, and who holds it where.

### Built-in lists

- **Consumables:** every consumable your characters hold.
- **Trade goods:** every trade good your characters hold.
- **Lumber** (Retail): every lumber type, with your **Essence of Lumber** on top. `/altinv lumber` opens it.

Lumber and Essence are Warbound, so they move freely between your characters. Each Essence trades for 20 lumber of any type at your neighborhood lumberjack (Lestia Goldenstrike or Xiz'ro), and the banner on top says how much lumber yours is worth and who holds it.

### Your own lists

1. Click **New list** and give it a name.
2. **Drop an item** on the box at the top to add it, or type its name, item ID or link and press Enter.
3. **Right-click a card** to take that item off the list.
4. **Right-click the list's name** to delete the list.

On any list, **hover** a card for the item's tooltip and **shift-click** it to link it in chat.
