# Play time

*How long you've played each character, and added up by class, role or faction.*

Open the window (minimap button or `/altinv search`) and pick the **Play time** tab.

![Play time by character, with the total on top](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/assets/img/shots/ai-playtime.webp)
*Play time by character, with the total on top*

- Each character's time played is shown as a bar in their class colour, with its share of the total. Hover a bar for details.
- Group the bars **by character**, **by class**, **by role** (from each character's current spec) or **by faction**.
- Your **total played** across every character is on the top right.

A character's time is read quietly when you log into it, so a character you haven't logged into since installing won't have a time yet. The bottom line says how many are waiting.

On Classic Era there are no specs, so the role grouping isn't there.
