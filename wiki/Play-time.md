# Play time

*How long you've played each character, and added up by class, role or faction.*

Open the window (minimap button or `/altinv search`) and pick the **Play time** tab.

- Each character's time played is shown as a bar, with its share of the total.
- Group the bars by **character**, **class**, **role** (from each character's current spec) or **faction**.

A character's time is read quietly when you log into it, so a character you haven't logged into since installing won't have a time yet.

On Classic Era there are no specs, so the role grouping isn't there.
