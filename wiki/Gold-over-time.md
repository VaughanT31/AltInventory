# Gold over time

*A chart of your gold over 30 days, 90 days or a year, for all of it, the Warband Bank or one character.*

Open the **Gold** tab in the Alt Inventory window, or type `/altinv gold`.

- The chart shows **all your gold** to start with: every character plus the Warband Bank.
- Pick **30 days**, **90 days** or **1 year** at the top. A year shows one bar per week.
- Hover a bar for that day's gold and how much it changed since the bar before. A bar is amber when your gold went down.
- The card on the top right shows the gold now and the change over the range.
- The list under the chart has **All your gold**, the **Warband Bank** (on Retail) and every character, most gold first, with their gold now and the change. Click one to chart it.

Gold is noted each day a character's gold is read: when you log in and whenever it changes. A character you don't log in on keeps its last amount, so the total still adds up. The chart starts from the day you installed 2.1.0, and a year of days is kept.
