# Gold and currencies

*Every character's gold at a glance, and who holds the currencies you can move between characters.*

### Gold

Hover the Alt Inventory button on the minimap. The tooltip lists each character's gold, the Warband Bank's gold and the total.

`/altinv` also prints every character with their gold, and when their bank and mailbox were last opened.

### Currencies

On Retail, Alt Inventory keeps the currencies each character holds that can be moved to another character with the game's **Transfer** option. Hover one of those currencies to see how much each character has, so you know who to transfer from.

You can also search for a currency by name: pick **Currencies** in the place filter of the search window.

Currencies shared by your whole account are the same on every character, so they aren't listed. On the Classic versions there is no Transfer and nothing is shared, so every currency counts per character.
