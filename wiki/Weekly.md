# Weekly

*Every character's Great Vault, Mythic+ keys this week and raid lockouts on one page.*

Open the **Weekly** tab in the Alt Inventory window, or type `/altinv weekly`.

Each character has a row:

- **Great Vault:** the raid, dungeon and world rows as three pips each. A pip is gold when that slot is unlocked, and dim gold when you've made a start on it. The count says how many of the three are unlocked.
- **Keys this week:** your three highest Mythic+ keys finished this week, and how many you've done.
- **Raid lockouts:** the first raid you're saved to, its difficulty and bosses down, with how many more and when it resets.

The cards on top add up **vault rewards waiting**, **keys this week** across your characters, **raid bosses down** and the time to the **weekly reset**.

Hover a character for everything: each vault slot with its reward item level (or how far along it is), every key this week, and every raid lockout with when it resets. Click a character to see their bags.

A character's week is read when you log in, after a boss kill or a key, and when the vault updates. After the weekly reset, characters that had vault slots unlocked show **vault rewards waiting** until you log in on them and collect.

On WoW Forever and the Classic versions there's no Great Vault or Mythic+, so the page shows raid lockouts only.
